Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains how Vouch Digital handles personal data about website visitors, people who enquire or use our free check, our clients and their staff, and our clients' customers. We do not sell personal data, and we never will.

1. Who we are

Vouch Digital is a trading name of Bolotin Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17405583). Registered office: 167–169 Great Portland Street, 5th Floor, London, W1W 5PF, United Kingdom. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), reference ZC234151.

For anything about your personal data, email privacy@vouchdigital.co.uk.

2. Our role

For data about website visitors, enquirers, people who use our free check, and our clients and their staff, we are the controller. For our clients' customers (for example, the people we send review requests to on a client's behalf), we act as a processor on our client's instructions. In that case, our client is the controller and is responsible for having a lawful basis to contact their customers.

5. International transfers

Some suppliers process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on protections recognised under UK law, such as the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, the International Data Transfer Agreement, or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep data

  • Enquiries and audit leads who don't become clients: up to 12 months after our last contact.
  • Client records: 6 years after the engagement ends, for legal and accounting reasons.
  • Our clients' customer data: deleted within 30 days of the engagement ending, unless the client asks us to return it first.

7. Cookies

Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Our booking calendar is provided by HighLevel and may set cookies needed for it to work. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers. If we add analytics or advertising tools in future, we will update this policy and ask for consent where needed.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, have it deleted, restrict or object to how we use it, ask us to transfer it, and withdraw consent where we rely on consent. To use any of these rights, email privacy@vouchdigital.co.uk. We will respond within one month.

If you are a customer of one of our clients, we may pass your request to that business, as they control your data.

9. Security

We use access controls and reputable suppliers to keep data secure, and we only give access to people who need it.

10. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.

We are a small business and are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer. Please send any privacy questions to privacy@vouchdigital.co.uk.

This policy explains how we handle personal data when you visit our website, use our free online audit, enquire about our services, become a client, or otherwise deal with us. It is written to meet our obligations under the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations.

2. The short version

  • We handle data about website visitors, people who enquire or use our free audit, our clients and their staff, and, on our clients' behalf, their own customers.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we never will.
  • We do not share personal data with data brokers or advertising networks.
  • Some of our suppliers are based outside the UK. Where that happens, we use the safeguards described in section 7.
  • You have rights over your data, set out in section 9, including the right to complain to the ICO.

3. What we collect

Website visitors

If you fill in a form on our website, we collect the information you give us: your name, your business name, your phone number, your email address and, if you add one, your message. Our website also records basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, which is generated automatically when any website is visited.

People who use our free online audit

If you run our free online audit, we collect the details you enter (your name, email address, phone number, business name and the search terms you choose) and the audit results, which are built from public information about your business, such as your Google Business Profile, your reviews, your website and your search rankings. If you tick the box to receive text messages, we also record that consent.

Enquirers and prospective clients

If you contact us, or we contact you about our services, we hold your name, business name, contact details and a record of our correspondence and calls.

We sometimes identify businesses that might benefit from our services using information published on public Google Business Profile listings and business websites, for example a business name, trading area, phone number, website and public review count. This is business contact information that the business has chosen to publish. We may use a research tool to collect this public information.

Clients

If you become a client, we hold your business details, the contact details of the people we deal with, records of the services we provide, correspondence and billing information. Card details are handled by Stripe and are not stored by us.

To deliver our services we may also be given manager access to your Google Business Profile, and we use your business details (such as your business name, address or service area, phone number, website, opening hours and photos) to manage your profile, build your website and create or update your listings on directories such as Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yell and Checkatrade.

Our clients' customers

When we run review request campaigns, we handle contact details of our clients' own customers, typically a name, a mobile number and/or an email address, and sometimes a job reference or completion date. These may come from a list the client gives us or, where the client asks us to set it up, from a connection to the client's job or invoicing software. We handle this data on our client's instructions. Section 4 explains what that means for you.

4. When we are not the responsible party

The law distinguishes between the organisation that decides how and why personal data is used (the "controller") and an organisation that only handles it on someone else's instructions (the "processor").

We are the controller for data about website visitors, people who use our free audit, enquirers, our clients and our clients' staff.

We are a processor for the customer contact data our clients give us, or ask us to collect from their own systems, so we can send review requests on their behalf. In that situation the client is the controller: it is their decision to run the campaign, their customer relationship, and their responsibility to have a lawful basis for contacting those customers. We act only on their documented instructions, under a written data processing agreement.

If you have received a review request from a business we work with and you want your data deleted or corrected, you can contact that business directly, or you can contact us at privacy@vouchdigital.co.uk and we will pass your request to them promptly and help them action it.

9. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete data, in certain circumstances;
  • ask us to restrict how we use your data, in certain circumstances;
  • object to us using your data where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • object to direct marketing at any time, with no exceptions;
  • ask us to transfer your data to you or another organisation, where it is held electronically and processed on the basis of consent or a contract; and
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on it.

To exercise any of these, email privacy@vouchdigital.co.uk. We will respond within one month. There is no charge.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

10. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website. We only use cookies that are strictly necessary for our website to work, for example to keep our forms and booking calendar secure and working properly. These do not require your consent.

Our booking calendar is provided by our CRM supplier, HighLevel, and our free online audit is provided by Merchynt; both may set strictly necessary cookies of their own. Our website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. If we introduce them in future, we will ask for your permission first and update this policy.

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings, though parts of the website may then not work correctly.

11. Security

We use access controls, strong authentication and encrypted connections to protect personal data, and we limit access to the people who need it. No system is completely secure, but we take our obligations seriously and will notify you and the ICO where we are legally required to do so following a breach.

12. Children

Our services are provided to businesses and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data about anyone under 18.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. If we make a significant change, we will tell our clients directly.

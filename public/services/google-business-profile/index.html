Questions about Google Business Profile

Do I lose control of my Google profile?

No. You stay the owner. We're added as a manager, which you can remove at any time in your Google settings.

I don't have a shop. Can I still show on Google Maps?

Yes. Most trades are set up as a “service-area business”, which hides your home address and shows the areas you cover instead.

Will this get me to the top of Google Maps?

We can't promise rankings, and anyone who does is guessing. What we can do is fix the things that hold profiles back and keep yours complete and active, month after month.

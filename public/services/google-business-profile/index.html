Frequently asked questions

Questions about google profile, local seo & ai search

No jargon, no promises we can't keep. Anything else, call 07984 003845.

Will this get me to the top of Google Maps?

We can't promise rankings, and anyone who does is guessing. What we can do is fix the things that hold profiles back and keep yours complete and active, month after month.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend me?

No, and be wary of anyone who says they can. We improve the information these tools rely on, which gives you the best chance of being mentioned.

Do I lose control of my Google profile?

No. You stay the owner. We're added as a manager, which you can remove at any time.

I work from home. Can I still show on Google Maps?

Yes. Most plumbers are set up as a “service-area business”, which hides your home address and shows the areas you cover instead.

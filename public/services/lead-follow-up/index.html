Frequently asked questions

Questions about lead follow-up & campaigns

Am I allowed to text my old customers?

Usually yes, if they're past customers who were given the chance to opt out and haven't. Every message includes an easy way to stop. We check this with you before anything is sent.

Won't customers find it annoying?

The messages are short, spaced out and written like you'd write them. Follow-ups stop the moment someone replies.

Do I have to use the campaigns?

No. They're there when you want more work, like a quiet week in summer. You decide when to press send.

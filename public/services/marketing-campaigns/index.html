Frequently asked questions

Questions about marketing campaigns

No jargon, no promises we can't keep. Anything else, call 07984 003845.

Am I allowed to text my old customers?

Usually yes, if they're past customers who were given the chance to opt out and haven't. Every message includes an easy way to stop. We check this with you before anything is sent.

Do I have to use the campaigns?

No. The service reminders run on their own, and the other campaigns are there when you want more work. You decide when to press send.

How many past customers do I need?

There's no minimum. Even a few dozen past customers is enough to make a campaign worthwhile.

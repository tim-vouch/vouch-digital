About us

We help plumbers get the reputation they've earned.

Vouch Digital is a small team run by Tim, working only with domestic plumbers and heating engineers across the UK.

Why we started

Good plumbers were losing jobs to busier-looking competitors

Most of the plumbers we speak to do brilliant work. Their customers are happy. But online, they look quieter than they are: a handful of old reviews, a half-finished Google profile, a website that's hard to use on a phone.

Meanwhile, homeowners are deciding who to call in a couple of minutes, from Google Maps and increasingly from ChatGPT. The business that looks most trusted gets the call, not always the one that does the best work.

We started Vouch Digital to fix that, and to do it the right way: genuine reviews from real customers, honest advice and no promises we can't keep.

How we work

What you can expect from us

  • Genuine reviews only

    We never write, buy, incentivise or filter reviews. Every customer is asked, and that's how UK law says it should be done.

  • Only plumbers

    We only work with domestic plumbers and heating engineers, so everything we do is built around how plumbing businesses win work.

  • Honest advice

    If something won't help your business, we'll tell you, even if that means you don't need us.

  • No lock-in

    Month to month. We'd rather keep you because it's working than because of a contract.

  • Your data, handled properly

    Registered with the ICO (ZC234151) and careful with your customers' details.

  • Anywhere in the UK

    Everything is done remotely over video call and phone, so it doesn't matter where you're based.

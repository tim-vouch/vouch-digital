How much does it cost?

Am I locked into a contract?

No. The monthly plan has no contract, so you can cancel anytime.

Do I own my domain?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your business's name, not ours.

How long does it take to get set up?

Your website is usually live within 3–5 days of us receiving your onboarding form. Then we walk you through everything on a 25-minute launch call.

Very little: a 15-minute first call, a short onboarding form, then the launch call. After that we run it in the background while you're out on jobs.
